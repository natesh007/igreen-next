## Redesign brief — identity refresh (started 2026-10-22, branch `redesign-identity`)

**Why:** A Sr UX designer said the site is recognisably AI-built (generic dark SaaS look). Goal: a distinct iGreen identity with **same content, routes, SEO and forms**. Principles taken from Impeccable / Taste Skill / motion-framer (nothing installed).

**Audience:** facility, hospitality, cold-chain, fire-safety and manufacturing operators; enterprise buyers and engineers evaluating an IoT/digital partner (India + US). They want proof of real deployments, not slogans.

**Proof points (use as typography, not decoration):** since 2008 (18+ yrs), 521+ projects, 232+ clients, 25 people, offices Hyderabad + Kakinada. Real products: smart building platform, hotel/FM, fire pump safety, cold storage monitoring, restaurant automation, visa platform, matchmaking AI, OilCop tank portal.

**Voice:** plain, specific, engineer-to-engineer. Numbers over adjectives.

**Do not:** claim ISO 9001 / IGBC (iGreen holds neither); invent clients; change URLs/titles/JSON-LD; break the contact/apply forms (reCAPTCHA v2 + Gmail SMTP).

**Audit of the pre-redesign site (the "tells" to remove)**
- Same recipe in every section: centred eyebrow + centred H2 + grid of identical rounded dark cards (Services, Process, Testimonials, About ×4, Portfolio).
- Poppins headings + Inter body everywhere (inline `fontFamily:'Poppins'` ~60×); gradient cyan→green headline text.
- One glowing cyan/green palette on near-black, alternating #0A0F1C/#060A12 bands; radial glows, noise, orbs.
- Rounded icon tile / generated SVG art above every card title; giant faint numerals on Process cards.
- Uniform fade-up `Reveal` on every block; gradient-border CTA card; three-up quote cards with initial avatars.
- No real imagery: no photos of deployments, no product screenshots.

**Assets the user will provide:** photos of real deployments; real product screenshots.
